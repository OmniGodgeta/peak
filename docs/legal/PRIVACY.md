**Draft — last updated 2026-09-10. Not yet in force.**

Peak is built so that your attention, your data, and your social graph belong to
you. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**You give us:**

- Account: email address, a hashed password (or a passkey), date of birth (used
  once to set 13+/teen status, then stored as an age band and the date).
- Profile: display name, handle, and anything else you choose to add (bio,
  avatar, links, pronouns, coarse location).
- Content: your posts, replies, reactions, messages, community activity, uploads.
- Reports you file and support messages you send.

**We collect automatically:**

- Basic delivery logs (IP address, timestamp, user agent) kept for up to
  〈N days〉 for security and abuse prevention, then deleted or truncated.
- Per-device settings you set (data-light mode, wellbeing settings) — these stay
  **on your device** and are not sent to us.

**We do not collect:** your contacts, your location beyond what you type, your
activity on other sites, or anything for advertising or ad targeting. Peak has no
third-party trackers or ad SDKs, and the dependency checklist in CI enforces
that.

## How we use it

- To run the features you use: show your posts to the audience you chose, deliver
  messages, run communities, power search over content you can already see.
- To keep the service safe: rate limiting, spam and abuse detection, responding
  to reports.
- To meet legal obligations (for example, responding to a valid legal request,
  or a mandatory child-safety report).

We do not profile you to rank your feed for engagement. Where ranking exists it is
optional and its rules are published.

## Encryption

Direct messages are moving to end-to-end encryption (see
[ENCRYPTION.md](../ENCRYPTION.md)). Where a conversation is end-to-end encrypted,
we cannot read its contents.

## Who we share with

- **Other users**, only as you direct — a post goes to the circles or community
  you addressed; nothing is public by default.
- **Service providers** strictly needed to operate Peak (hosting, email
  delivery), under contract, only for that purpose.
- **Authorities**, when we receive a valid legal order or where a mandatory
  report is required (e.g. child sexual abuse material).
- **Never** advertisers, data brokers, or AI training pipelines.

If you enable federation, content you send to another instance is governed by
that instance's policies.

## Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can:

- **Access and export** everything (Settings → Your data → Export).
- **Correct** your profile at any time.
- **Delete** your account. After a 30-day grace period your data is erased,
  except backups that age out on our normal 〈N-day〉 cycle and records we must
  keep by law.
- **Object** to a specific processing activity by contacting us.

If you are in the EEA/UK, our legal bases are: performance of the contract (the
service), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention), legal obligation,
and consent (anything optional). You can complain to your local data protection
authority.

## Children

Peak is for ages 13+. Under-18 accounts default to private and non-discoverable
with restricted messaging and no behavioural ranking. We do not knowingly create
accounts for under-13s; if we learn of one we remove it.
