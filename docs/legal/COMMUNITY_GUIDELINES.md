# Community Guidelines

**Draft — last updated 2026-09-10. Not yet in force.**

Peak is a place for real connection between people. These guidelines apply
everywhere on Peak. They are part of the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md). Community
moderators may set additional rules for their own communities, but not looser
ones.

## The line

**Not allowed anywhere, ever:**

- **Child sexual abuse material or the sexualisation of minors** — in any form,
  including drawings, AI-generated images, and "aged-up" claims. Reported to the
  authorities; account removed.
- **Credible threats of violence** or incitement to violence against a person or
  group.
- **Terrorist or violent-extremist content** that promotes or celebrates such
  acts.
- **Coordinated harassment** — organising or joining a pile-on against someone.
- **Doxxing** — posting someone's private information (home address, phone,
  documents, precise location) without their consent.
- **Non-consensual intimate imagery**, and threats to share it.
- **Content that promotes self-harm or suicide**, or encourages eating disorders.
- **Impersonation** intended to deceive.
- **Spam, scams, and malware.**

## Hateful conduct

Attacking people because of race, ethnicity, national origin, caste, religion,
disability, disease, age, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation is not
allowed. Discussing these topics is fine; dehumanising people over them is not.

## Adult content

Consensual adult nudity and sexual content is allowed **only** in communities
marked 18+ and behind a content warning. It must never appear in a
non-marked space, on a profile, or where a minor could reasonably encounter it.
Teen accounts cannot see 18+ communities.

## Automation and authenticity

- Bots and automated posting must be clearly labelled.
- No fake engagement, no bought followers, no sock-puppet networks.
- No large-scale scraping.

## Misinformation

We remove misinformation that causes real-world harm (e.g. false claims that
suppress votes, or dangerous health claims presented as fact). We do not
adjudicate ordinary disagreement or opinion.

## How enforcement works

Depending on severity, we may: add a label, limit reach, remove content, restrict
an account, or remove it. We favour the lightest tool that resolves the problem.

- Every site-level action is recorded, and you can ask why.
- You can **appeal** any action to 〈appeals contact〉; we aim to respond within
  〈N〉 days.
- Community-level actions are shown in that community's public moderation log.

## Reporting

Use the **Report** option on any post, profile, or community. Reports about child
safety, credible violence, or self-harm go straight to site staff and are
prioritised. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency
services.
